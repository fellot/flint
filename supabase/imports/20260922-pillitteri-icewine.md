# Pillitteri Reserve Vidal Icewine 2015

The companion SQL adds **one bottle** to cellar 1. It leaves price and personal ratings NULL and location unassigned. It runs independently of previous imports and safely skips this batch ID on rerun. Bottle size has not been assumed.

- **Identity:** Canada, Ontario, VQA Niagara-on-the-Lake; 100% Vidal; sweet white dessert wine, stored as `Sweet` for the application's style filters. This is the Reserve bottling, not the standard Vidal Icewine.
- **Exact-vintage review:** [Wine Enthusiast](https://www.wineenthusiast.com/buying-guide/pillitteri-2015-reserve-icewine-vidal-niagara-on-the-lake/), Paul Gregutt, July 2020: **91 points**, neutral-oak ageing, 232 g/L residual sugar and 10.5% ABV. The review describes a dense, sweet wine with vanilla, candied-fruit and maple character.
- **Other awards:** [IWSC 2018](https://www.iwsc.net/results/detail/84524/pillitteri-estate-2015-reserve-vidal-icewine) gives Silver Outstanding; [IWSC 2019](https://www.iwsc.net/results/detail/92918/pillitteri-reserve-vidal-icewine-2015) gives Silver. Both identify vintage 2015 and 100% Vidal, but list **11% ABV**. The discrepancy is retained in notes; the physical bottle is the best source for your lot. No exact-vintage WA/JS score was verified, so those fields remain NULL. WE 91 and IWSC awards are in notes, not the WA/JS-only rating column.
- **Window / peak:** **2026–2035 / 2028**, editorial planning estimates for a well-stored mature bottle. [Producer Reserve-series information](https://www.pillitteri.com/our-wines/) suggests 20+ years of ageability generally. This does not guarantee the condition of a particular 2015 bottle. Peak is a planning target, not a verified single year.
- **Pairings:** blue cheese, aged cheddar, or a citrus/fruit dessert less sweet than the wine; suggested meal is blue cheese with walnuts and pear, or a lightly sweetened lemon tart. Serving at 10–12°C is a suggestion consistent with the [2015 merchant listing](https://www.vino.com/en/dettaglio/niagara-on-the-lake-vqa-vidal-icewine-reserve-pillitteri-2015.html).
